# Code of conduct

Nudge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment of
any kind isn't tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through GitHub. All reports are
reviewed and kept confidential.
